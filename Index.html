How to Identify Original vs Fake ONE8 Shoes?

There are many fake One8 shoes in the market. Here is how you can identify the original one:

1. Check the ONE8 Logo: In original shoes, the stitching and finishing of the logo is perfect. Fake shoes have uneven logo and loose threads.

2. Check the Box: Original ONE8 shoes always come with a PUMA box, with a barcode, MRP, and size chart. No box = 99% fake.

3. Check the Price: Original ONE8 shoes never come for Rs 1500. If someone is selling ONE8 for Rs 1500 - Rs 2000, it is 100% a copy. Original price starts from Rs 2999.

4. Ask for the Bill: Original sellers always provide a bill. We provide a 100% original bill with every pair.

Final Tip: Buy only from a trusted store.
